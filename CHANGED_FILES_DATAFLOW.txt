Behavior:
- Plan steps declare explicit input/output fact keys.
- ExecutionContext accumulates reusable facts across step attempts.
- ContextComposer supplies only facts requested by the current step.
- Missing inputs block the step and invoke Recovery before an LLM call.
- Recovery-inserted steps become explicit dependencies of the blocked step.
- Active semantic steps no longer receive conversation/execution-history blobs.
- edit-file publishes a deterministic applied-change result after ChangeExecutor succeeds.
- No asynchronous execution was introduced.
