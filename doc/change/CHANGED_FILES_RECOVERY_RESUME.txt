Behavior:
- planner receives explicit supported step types, descriptions, and hard limits from StepRegistry;
- exhausted/failed plan steps invoke one focused recovery prompt before asking for human help;
- recovery can retry, insert focused steps, skip a genuinely unnecessary step, request human help, or fail;
- paused executions retain TaskPlan, current step, history, tool evidence, and applied changes in memory;
- natural "продолжи" / "continue" resumes the same execution instead of creating a new task;
- "продолжи, <hint>" and /resume <hint> feed a human hint into recovery;
- /stop discards the paused execution;
- global maxSteps is treated as a safety segment limit with a minimum of 20; reaching it pauses instead of failing;
- automatic recovery is limited per step; human resume is limited to 3 resumes.
