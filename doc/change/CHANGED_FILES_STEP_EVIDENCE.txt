Behavior changes:
- Semantic steps return explicit stepResult { goalSatisfied, findings, evidence, missing }.
- Active plan step is passed explicitly to the model; future plan goals are not part of the model's routing responsibility.
- Completed step evidence is compactly carried forward between semantic steps.
- Search/understand/prepare-change/review/verify only complete when their active goal is explicitly satisfied.
- Recovery receives concrete missing evidence and completed evidence.
- Duplicate/no-progress recovery expansion is rejected and execution pauses for a human hint instead of growing the plan indefinitely.
- Console now shows short factual step results; verbose mode also shows evidence sources.
